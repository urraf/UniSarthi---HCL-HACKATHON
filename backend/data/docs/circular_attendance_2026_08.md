# Circular No. ACAD-2026-08: Revision of Minimum Attendance Requirement

Office of the Dean (Academics). Dated 20 July 2026. Effective from 1 August 2026.

## 1 Revised minimum attendance
With effect from 1 August 2026, a student must have a minimum of 80% attendance in each course to be allowed to appear in the end-semester examination of that course. This supersedes clause 7.2 of the Academic Regulations 2024.

## 2 Condonation
Condonation of attendance under clause 7.4 of the Academic Regulations 2024 remains unchanged.

## 3 Applicability
This circular applies to all programmes and all batches from the odd semester of the academic year 2026-27.
