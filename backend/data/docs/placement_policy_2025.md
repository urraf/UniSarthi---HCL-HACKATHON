# Training and Placement Policy (Sample)

Training and Placement Office. Version 2.0. Effective from 1 July 2025.

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets out who may register for campus placements and the rules that apply during the placement season.

## 3 Eligibility for registration

### 3.1 Minimum CGPA
A student must have a CGPA of at least 6.5 at the time of registration to register for campus placements.

### 3.2 Backlogs
A student must have no active backlogs (zero courses with result FAIL, ABSENT or DETAINED that have not been cleared) at the time of registration.

### 3.3 Final-year students
Registration is open to students of the 5th semester and above.

## 4 Rules during placements

### 4.1 One student, one job
A student who accepts an offer is not allowed to sit for further placement drives, except for offers with a salary at least 1.5 times the accepted offer.

### 4.2 Attendance at drives
A student who registers for a drive and is absent without informing the Training and Placement Office is debarred from the next two drives.
