# Circular No. PLACE-2026-11: Revised CGPA Criterion for Placements (Sample)

Training and Placement Office. Dated 1 October 2026. Effective from 1 December 2026.

## 1 Revised minimum CGPA
With effect from 1 December 2026, students of batch 2024 and later must have a CGPA of at least 7.0 to register for campus placements. This supersedes clause 3.1 of the Training and Placement Policy 2025 for these batches.

## 2 Other conditions
All other conditions of the Training and Placement Policy 2025 remain unchanged.
