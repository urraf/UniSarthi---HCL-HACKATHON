# Academic Regulations for B.Tech Programmes (Sample)

Office of the Dean (Academics). Version 3.1. Effective from 1 July 2024.

## 7 Attendance

### 7.1 Counting of attendance
Attendance is counted separately for each course, from the first day of teaching to the last day of teaching of the semester. Lectures, tutorials and laboratory sessions are all counted.

### 7.2 Minimum attendance
A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be allowed to appear in the end-semester examination of that course.

### 7.3 Detention
A student whose attendance in a course is below the minimum required under clause 7.2, after any condonation under clause 7.4, shall be declared DETAINED in that course and shall not appear in its end-semester examination.

### 7.4 Condonation
The Dean (Academics) may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 5 percentage points for medical reasons, subject to clause 7.2, on submission of a medical certificate within 7 days of the student rejoining classes.

## 9 Evaluation

### 9.1 Distribution of marks
Each theory course is evaluated out of 100 marks: 40 marks of internal assessment and 60 marks of end-semester examination.

### 9.2 Pass criterion
A student passes a course if the total of internal and end-semester marks is at least 40% of the maximum marks.

### 9.3 Letter grades

| Total marks (out of 100) | Grade | Grade points |
|---|---|---|
| 90 - 100 | O | 10 |
| 80 - 89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70 - 79 | A | 8 |
| 60 - 69 | B+ | 7 |
| 50 - 59 | B | 6 |
| 40 - 49 | C | 5 |
| Below 40 | F | 0 |

## 10 Supplementary Examination

### 10.1 Schedule
Supplementary examinations are held once a year, in July, for courses of both odd and even semesters.

### 10.2 Eligibility
A student whose result in a course is FAIL or ABSENT may appear in the supplementary examination of that course. A student declared DETAINED in a course under clause 7.3 is not eligible and must re-register for the course.

### 10.3 Procedure to apply
1. Apply on the student portal within 7 days of the declaration of results.
2. Select the courses for which you wish to appear.
3. Pay the supplementary examination fee of Rs 500 per course online.
4. Download the admit card from the portal 3 days before the examination.

### 10.4 Marks after supplementary
Internal assessment marks are carried forward. Only the end-semester component is re-examined.
