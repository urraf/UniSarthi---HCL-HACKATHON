# Student Help-desk FAQ (Sample)

Student Help Desk. Updated 15 September 2026.

## Q1 Where do I see my attendance?
Attendance for each course is shown on the student portal under Academics, then Attendance. It is updated every Monday.

## Q2 Who do I contact about a wrong attendance entry?
Contact the course instructor first. If it is not corrected within a week, write to the Head of Department.

## Q3 How much attendance do I need?
65% attendance is enough to sit the end-semester exams.

## Q4 When is the hostel fee due?
The hostel fee for the odd semester is due by 31 July and for the even semester by 31 December. A late fee of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date.

## Q5 How do I get a bonafide certificate?
Apply on the student portal under Services. The certificate is issued by the Registrar's office within 3 working days.
